# Content overview and publishing disclosures

The content overview separates persisted publication facts from the current publishing task. A task cannot promote or replace the publication fact. Unknown submission results require platform verification; the overview offers navigation to existing management controls rather than a retry action.

Publishing and account-check disclosures are local display state. Hiding them preserves inputs and in-flight operations; changing the selected content closes both. Platform alerts move focus into the matching management row. Backend authorization and publication records remain owned by the existing services.
