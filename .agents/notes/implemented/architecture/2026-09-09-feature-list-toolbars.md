# Feature-list toolbars

Feature-list toolbars omit record totals and content or knowledge creation actions. Group counts, overview metrics and activity badges belong to their existing surfaces. Knowledge browsing retains its central overview and selectable records.

Inspiration keeps history filtering separate from starting research. The shared section header owns filter disclosure, clearing and focus restoration. The history trigger pairs the search icon with Search text and retains the accessible name Search history. Research entry belongs to the central workbench. Refresh remains available when the filter is closed and is disabled during a refresh.

DOM tests cover history filtering, Escape and Clear focus restoration, absence of a sidebar research action, English labels, content archive options and knowledge selection. The visual rules live in DESIGN.md and the user flow lives in docs/inspiration.md.
