# Operation icons

The client embeds sixteen operation PNGs alongside the existing feature family. WorkbenchIcon uses semantic names; button labels, event handlers, loading states and confirmation flows remain owned by their controls. Refresh and retry share one symbol; clear-search, remove-record and stop-work use distinct symbols. External-link icons decorate links without altering destinations.

Toolbar icons render at 24 px. Compact sidebar branding remains visible until the expand action is hovered or focused. Platform marks and small disclosure arrows retain their existing representations. The placement and asset provenance are documented in docs/iconography.md and the asset manifest.
